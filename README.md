# Hamro Kedarsyn Portal

A modern digital portal for **Kedarsyun Rural Municipality (केदारस्युँ गाउँपालिका)**, designed to provide citizens and visitors with easy access to municipal information, services, tourism, news, events, media, and other public resources.

## 🌐 About the Project

Hamro Kedarsyn Portal is a web-based municipal information and content management platform.

The portal provides:

- 🏛️ Municipal information
- 👥 Local representatives and administration
- 🏔️ Tourist destinations
- 🛕 Religious places
- 🌿 Natural resources
- 🎭 Culture and traditions
- 🍲 Local food and products
- 🏡 Homestays
- 📰 News and notices
- 📅 Events and programs
- 📷 Photo gallery
- 🎥 Video gallery
- 🔎 Search functionality
- 🔐 Administrative dashboard
- 🗄️ Supabase-powered backend

## ✨ Features

### Public Website

- Responsive and modern UI
- Nepali language support
- Municipality information
- Tourism information
- Local products and culture
- News and events
- Photo and video galleries
- Search functionality
- Contact and about pages

### Admin Dashboard

The administration panel provides content management functionality for:

- Tourism destinations
- Religious places
- Natural resources
- Culture
- Local information
- Local products
- Homestays
- News
- Events
- Photos
- Videos

### Backend

The project uses **Supabase** for:

- Database
- Authentication
- Storage
- Content management
- Image management
